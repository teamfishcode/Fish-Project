About Zebrafish

Zebrafish are commonly used in scientific research because they are small, develop quickly and their larvae are transparent. This makes it easier to observe organs such as the heart.

Our Project

The aim of this project is to develop an image analysis method that can automatically measure the heart rate of freely swimming zebrafish larvae from high-speed video.

We are particularly interested in measuring heart rate without tranquillising the zebrafish. The project will involve image processing, segmentation and analysis of the heartbeat signal.
